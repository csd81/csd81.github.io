# §2.8 Multivariable Calculus Review

> 🚧 **Status:** content stub. No widget; pure background material. Copy prose from `/home/csd81/Desktop/02/Chapter_2_full.md` §2.8.

For $f \in C^1$: gradient $f'(\mathbf{x}) = (\partial f/\partial x_1, \ldots, \partial f/\partial x_n)^T$.

For $f \in C^2$: Hessian $f''(\mathbf{x})$ — the $n \times n$ matrix of second partials.

For $\mathbf{f}\colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$: Jacobian $\mathbf{f}'(\mathbf{x})$ — the $n \times n$ matrix of $\partial f_i / \partial x_j$.

Second-order Taylor:
$$f(\mathbf{x}) \approx f(\mathbf{a}) + f'(\mathbf{a})^T(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{a}) + \tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{a})^T f''(\mathbf{a})(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{a}).$$
