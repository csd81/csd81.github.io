# §2.4 Method of False Position

> 🚧 **Status:** content stub. **Widget to build:** `FalsePositionStepper`. Copy prose from `/home/csd81/Desktop/02/Chapter_2_full.md` §2.4.

At step $k$, instead of the midpoint we use the secant intercept
$$p_k = a_k - f(a_k)\,\frac{a_k - b_k}{f(a_k) - f(b_k)} \tag{2.6}$$
and keep whichever half-interval $[a_k, p_k]$ or $[p_k, b_k]$ has a sign change.

The two worked examples in the book — $e^x - 2\cos x$ on $[0,1]$ (8 steps, much faster than bisection's 16) and the same equation on $[0,4]$ (51 steps, much slower than bisection's 18) — illustrate that taking the function's shape into account *can* speed things up, but doesn't always.
