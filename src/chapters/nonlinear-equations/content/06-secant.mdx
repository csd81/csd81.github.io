# §2.6 Secant Method

> 🚧 **Status:** content stub. **Widget to build:** `SecantStepper`. Copy prose from `/home/csd81/Desktop/02/Chapter_2_full.md` §2.6.

$$p_{k+1} = p_k - \frac{p_k - p_{k-1}}{f(p_k) - f(p_{k-1})} f(p_k). \tag{2.10}$$

The secant method is a Newton iteration where $f'(p_k)$ is replaced by the difference quotient $(f(p_k) - f(p_{k-1}))/(p_k - p_{k-1})$. Two-step, not one-step.

<TheoremBox kind="theorem" number="2.36">
  If $p$ is a simple root of $f$, then the secant method converges locally to $p$ of order $\alpha = (1 + \sqrt{5})/2 \approx 1.618$.
</TheoremBox>

The order is genuinely irrational — a striking demonstration that order-of-convergence theory for multi-step iterations differs from the integer-only world of fixed-point iterations.
