# §2.5 Newton's Method

> 🚧 **Status:** content stub. **Widgets to build:** `NewtonStepper`, `NewtonArctanDemo`. Copy prose from `/home/csd81/Desktop/02/Chapter_2_full.md` §2.5.

Replace $f$ near $p_0$ by its first-order Taylor approximation $f(p_0) + f'(p_0)(x - p_0)$, set it to zero, solve for $x$. Iterate from there.

$$p_{k+1} = p_k - \frac{f(p_k)}{f'(p_k)}. \tag{2.7}$$

<TheoremBox kind="theorem" number="2.23">
  Let $f \in C^2[a,b]$ and $p \in (a,b)$ with $f(p) = 0$ and $f'(p) \neq 0$. Then Newton's method converges locally to $p$.
</TheoremBox>

The pathological example $f(x) = 0.5 \arctan x$ has $f'(0) = 0.5 \neq 0$, so Newton's method converges locally — but for $|p_0| > p^* \approx 1.3918$ it diverges, and at $p_0 = p^*$ it cycles $p^*, -p^*, p^*, \ldots$. The `NewtonArctanDemo` widget visualises all three regimes with a single slider.
