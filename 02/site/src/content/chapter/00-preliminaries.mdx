<TheoremBox kind="theorem" number="2.4" title="Lagrange's Mean Value Theorem">
  Let $f \in C^1[a,b]$. Then there exists $\xi \in (a,b)$ such that
  $$f(b) - f(a) = f'(\xi)(b - a).$$
</TheoremBox>

<TheoremBox kind="theorem" number="2.5" title="Taylor's Theorem">
  Let $f \in C^{n+1}[a,b]$ and $x_0 \in (a,b)$. For every $x \in (a,b)$, $x \neq x_0$, there exists $\xi \in \langle x, x_0\rangle$ such that
  $$f(x) = T_n(x) + \frac{f^{(n+1)}(\xi)}{(n+1)!}(x - x_0)^{n+1}.$$
</TheoremBox>
