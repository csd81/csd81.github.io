# §2.3 Bisection Method

<TheoremBox kind="theorem" number="2.16">
  Let $f \in C[a,b]$ and $f(a)f(b) < 0$. Then the bisection sequence $p_k$ converges to a root $p$ of $f$, and
  $$|p_k - p| \leq \frac{b - a}{2^{k+1}}. \tag{2.4}$$
</TheoremBox>
