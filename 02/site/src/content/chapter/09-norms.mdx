# §2.9 Vector and Matrix Norms and Convergence

The three usual vector $p$-norms — $\|\mathbf{x}\|_1$, $\|\mathbf{x}\|_2$, $\|\mathbf{x}\|_\infty$ — and the induced matrix norms $\|\mathbf{A}\|_1$ (max column sum) and $\|\mathbf{A}\|_\infty$ (max row sum).

Mnemonic: subscript 1 looks like a vertical bar → sum *down* the column. Subscript $\infty$ looks horizontal → sum *across* the row.
