### Section 3.5: Positive Definite Matrices & Gauss-Jordan Elimination

* **[[01:14:20](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hp9QV-d1Wj4&t=4460)]** Let's continue with a few theoretical remarks. First, introduce the notion of a **diagonally dominant matrix**. $$|a_{ii}| > \sum_{j \neq i} |a_{ij}|$$
